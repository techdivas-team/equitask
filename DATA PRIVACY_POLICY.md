1. Introduction
EquiTask is committed to protecting your personal data while providing a safe, inclusive, and empowering experience for all users, including disabled and neurodivergent individuals. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data, including data processed by AI features in the app.
By using EquiTask, you consent to the practices described in this policy.


2. Personal Data We Collect
We may collect the following personal data for the purpose of providing our services:

Account Information – name, email, password (hashed), role (User, Manager, Admin)

Task Information – tasks you create, deadlines, milestones

Proof Submissions – uploaded images, audio recordings, text submissions

Usage Data – app usage, AI feature interactions, session logs

Accessibility Preferences – visual, audio, or interface settings to support neurodivergent and disabled users

Important: All sensitive data, including passwords, is encrypted and securely stored.


4. Use of AI Features
EquiTask includes AI features to simplify task management, assist focus, and provide adaptive learning experiences.

AI uses your task data only to provide suggestions and summaries.

AI does not store your personal identifiers permanently.

AI outputs are designed to respect accessibility preferences and assist neurodivergent users.

All AI processing is encrypted in transit and monitored for privacy compliance.


6. Purpose of Data Collection
We collect your data to:

Enable account creation and login

Manage tasks and deadlines

Provide AI-powered task simplification and progress insights

Support accessibility features for users with disabilities or neurodivergence

Monitor app security and prevent unauthorized access


8. Data Sharing & Third Parties
No sale of data: We never sell or rent your personal data.
Service providers: Limited data may be shared with trusted partners (e.g., cloud storage providers) for app functionality only, under strict confidentiality and encryption.
Legal compliance: We may share data to comply with laws or protect user safety.


9. Data Retention
Personal data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the service.
Users may request deletion of their data anytime via the app or support contact.
Proof data and AI usage logs are anonymized where possible to protect privacy.


10. Data Security Measures
We implement industry-standard security practices:

Encryption of sensitive data at rest and in transit

Secure password hashing (bcrypt/Argon2)

Role-based access control (RBAC)

AI input sanitization and secure processing

Regular security audits and vulnerability testing


12. User Rights
You have the right to:
Access your personal data
Correct inaccurate data
Request deletion of your data
Restrict or object to processing
Withdraw consent to AI processing
